Found a bug, want a feature, or something on the table does not read right? Write to jeffryariasp@gmail.com. Include your phone model and what you tapped.

Quick answers

How do I change life or time?

Life mode: tap the top half of your seat for plus, the bottom half for minus. Hold to ramp. Clock mode: long-press the center hub to edit the time bank, and open the settings gear on the home screen for player count, seating and starting values.

How do I record commander damage or counters?

Tap the detail icon in the corner of your seat. The Commander tab tracks damage from each opponent (it also moves your life), the Counters tab holds poison, energy, experience, rad, treasure and commander tax.

A player was eliminated by mistake.

Tap Revive on the shroud. The seat comes back one step off lethal with everything else untouched.

Where are mana and dice?

Tap the hourglass hub in the center of the table. The flask opens the shared mana pool, the dice opens the tray with coins, dice, a random seat and a random opponent.

How do I rename a player?

Tap the name pill on the seat. Names are saved and shared by both modes.

The screen turns off mid game.

Tempo keeps the screen awake while a game is open. If it still sleeps, check that Low Power Mode or a battery saver is not forcing a shorter timeout.
