Privacy policy

Tempo does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Everything the app knows lives on your device.

Effective date: September 29, 2026. Applies to Tempo for iOS and Android, published by Jeffry Arias.

What the app stores

Tempo saves your table setup on your device so it is ready the next time you open the app: player count, seating arrangement, starting life, starting time, and each seat’s name, colour and card art. Players you choose to save (name, colour and card art) are stored the same way, and the card images you pick are kept in the app’s own storage so they show without a connection. This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Card art search

When you search for card art, the app sends only the text you typed to the public Scryfall API and downloads the card images you choose from Scryfall. No account, identifier or other data is sent with it. Scryfall is an independent service with its own privacy policy. Searching is the only time Tempo uses the network; everything else works offline.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign in and no email address.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No network requests other than the card art search described above.
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Permissions

Tempo keeps the screen awake while a game is open so the table can read it. That is the only system capability it uses.

Children

Tempo is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever changes what the app stores or collects, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will move.
