Put the phone in the middle of the table. Play.

Tempo is the table companion for Magic: The Gathering. Every player gets their own seat, rotated to face them. Life, commander damage, counters, mana and a chess clock for every seat.

Life mode

Tap to move life, hold to ramp. Delta chips show the table what just happened. 20, 30, 40 or custom starting life for 2 to 4 players.

Chess clock

A time bank per player. Tap your seat to start your clock and pause everyone else. Low time glows ember; zero is out.

Commander ready

Commander damage per opponent, poison, energy, experience, rad, treasure and commander tax. Monarch and initiative badges.

Mana and storm

A shared mana pool with official mana glyphs and a storm count kept outside your mana total.

Dice and randomizers

Coin, d4 to d20, custom dice, random seat and random opponent. Natural 20s get a gold burst.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet needed. The screen stays awake for the whole game.
